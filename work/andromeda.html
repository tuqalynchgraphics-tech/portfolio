Andromeda AI
Brand identity · Type · Art direction

Andromeda AI is a bespoke AI automation studio I co-founded, where I design both the client-facing products we build and the brand and website that represent the studio itself. I built the identity around the Andromeda name itself, using one saturated violet (#5D22D6), a halftone dot texture standing in for stellar dust, and a sharp four-point sparkle mark paired with Cabinet Grotesk and Erode type to give the brand a bolder, more graphic feel.
