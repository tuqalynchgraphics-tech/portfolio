About me

Hey, I'm Tuqa

I enjoy working across UX/UI, branding, and digital storytelling, and I’m always curious about how emerging tools can open up new ways for people to connect with businesses online. Combined with genuine ability across brand, editorial, motion, and interactive design, that means I can take a project from concept through to a working, shipped product.

Working with clients, I value communication above almost everything else. Iterating openly, with continuous feedback is how I get to work a client actually believes in.

Outside of client projects, I'm currently exploring creative technologies like TouchDesigner, experimenting with immersive and interactive design. I love learning new skills and value work that provides the opportunity to do so.

I'm currently open to taking on new projects. Drop me an email or text and we can discuss your design problem or vision. Let's work together! :)
